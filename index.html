$19B

$19 billion in one day. Nobody saw the chain.

On October 10, 2025, falling prices forced traders who had borrowed to bet on crypto to sell. Their selling pushed prices lower and forced out the next group. Each exchange saw only its own customers, so nobody saw the chain reaction coming. As more trading is handed to similar AI models, crowded moves like this only get bigger.
