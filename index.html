We don't need anyone's private book.

Mechanical flows — leveraged ETFs, volatility targets, trend signals, dealer hedging — follow published rules. We compute them from public aggregates.

Discretionary crowding is inferred from market footprints and reported as a range, with the assumptions that drive it.

With permission, client data sharpens the picture — only ever in aggregated, anonymised form.

Calibration on historical event windows is in progress; out-of-sample tests against simpler baselines come next.

05 / Research Line 2 · Research

Toward a financial world model

Long term, MicroWorld becomes a participant-level simulator of financial markets: structural institutional agents, learned behaviour, population-weighted LLM archetypes including retail crowds, and an order-book layer — continuously re-estimated from live data, the way weather forecasts are. The goal: test decisions, policies and shocks before the market does.

The open-source MicroWorld repository is our research lineage, separate from the product engine.
